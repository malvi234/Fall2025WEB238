My Skincare Routine

Step 1: Cleanser
For the first step, I will usually do double cleansing. I first go in with the Anu Cleansing oil to remove dirt, makeup, and any excess oil or dead skin off of my face. Then I will go in with my Dokdo Cleanser and lather it in my hands until it turns to foam to remove any excess dirt from my face.
Step 2: Toner
I use the Klairs toner for sensitive skin. This helps balance your pH levels and brings back hydration to the skin.
Step 3: Essence
For this step I use the Klair Vitamin C serum. This helps improve skin tone and will brighten the skin.
Step 4: Moisturizer
For my last step I will use my Illiyoon cream to moisturize my skin. This can help repair your skin barrier and is great for sensitive skin.

⭐ Sungboon Editor Collagen Mask

This is super hydrating, ani-wrinkle and lifting, and provides real collagen to the skin.


What is Double Cleansing?

Double cleansing uses an oil cleanser first to remove makeup and sunscreen, followed by a water-based cleanser to clean the skin thoroughly.
